Auditor's note on additive AME effects reproducing dM = M_i − M_j: v2 does not model (dM, dR) as an outcome. The outcome is the chosen target; dM and dR are covariates inside a quadratic utility, so sender and receiver effects (a_i, b_j) cannot reproduce the outcome algebraically.

## mu intercept (2026-10-23; PI-approved)

Problem. In both Stan programs the ideal target position was `mu[n] = X[n] beta + a_i` with `a_i ~ MVN(0, Omega)` mean zero and no intercept column in X. The two conflict-history columns were centred but `cowmaj_tm1` and `dem_tm1` were uncentred 0/1, so the population-average ideal gap was pinned at 0 for non-major non-democracies. Since x is the initiator-minus-target gap, that intercept is the average preferred gap (bully > 0, peer about 0, challenger < 0), the paper's central quantity, and the model could only express it through the state offsets a_i, which the mean-zero prior shrinks back towards 0.

| change | detail |
|---|---|
| Stan | `row_vector[D] mu0` added to both programs (length 1 in the m / r models through `D`). `mu = mu0 + X beta + a_i` in the reduce_sum partial sum, `Mu` in generated quantities, `mu_state` at `x_ref` and `mu_event`. Prior `mu0 ~ normal(0, prior_mu0_sd)`, `prior_mu0_sd` is data. No profile() inside the partial sum. |
| R data | `build_stan_data(prior_mu0_sd = 2)` passes the prior sd; every caller (fit script, sim, tests, measurement tests) goes through it. `assemble_cell.R` centres `cowmaj_tm1` and `dem_tm1` like the conflict-history columns (dM_i, dR_i untouched), so mu0 is the ideal gap at zero power change and average controls; alpha0 in stage 1 is the log-odds at the same point. |
| R reimplementations | `stage2_loglik_R()`, `measurement_tests.R::extract_draws()` / `stage2_scores()` and `sim/simulate.R` (new `mu0` argument, default 0; `truth$mu0`, `truth$mu_state_ref0 = mu0 + a_i`) include mu0 and stop if it is missing. `02_fit_model.R`, `run_sim.R`, `run_sim_1d.R`, `compare_refit.R` summarise mu0. |
| Tests | The fixed-draw likelihood tests use a non-zero mu0 (0.7, -0.4) and also check `mu_event`; the scorer tests use (0.6, -0.3) and assert both reimplementations fail without mu0. New `test_mu0_recovery.R` fits M1b to data simulated with mu0 = (0.8, -0.5) (S = 20, 12 years) and requires the 90% interval to cover the truth. |

Not changed. Other priors, data coding, sampling settings, choice sets. Existing simulation results in `sim/SIM_REPORT.md` predate mu0 and were not rerun here.

## Power measures and target changes (2026-10-23; PI-approved)

Problem. M and R were within-year z-scores of log CINC share and IGO PageRank, and the change variables dM_h, dR_h were differences of those z-scores. Three consequences. (1) The changes moved with the growth of the system (correlation with the 3-year log growth in the number of states +0.29 for M and +0.17 for R at h = 3), because a state's z-score shifts whenever entrants change the year's mean and spread. (2) The changes had SD 0.11 and 0.25 in raw units, so the N(0, 1) per-unit priors on β and α dominated the data. (3) The target's own power change was absent from stage 2.

| change | detail |
|---|---|
| Positions | M = log(CINC share) and R_pr = log(PageRank), without within-year standardisation. Both shares sum to one over the system in a year, so M and R are log shares of the system total (world capabilities, network centrality) and the stage-2 gaps dM = M_i − M_j and dR_pr = R_pr_i − R_pr_j are log ratios between the two states, not rescaled. A new guard stops the build when PageRank is non-finite or ≤ 0, mirroring the CINC check. The `assert_standardized` checks on M and R_pr became finiteness checks. R_eig is untouched (still a within-year z-score; not in the main grid). |
| Changes | One state-level table per lag (`changes_lag{h}.rds`, `build_change_table()` in `00_helpers.R`). For each state in the system at t with positions at t−1 and t−1−h, the raw change is level(t−1) − level(t−1−h) in log units (`dM_raw_h`, `dRpr_raw_h`, calendar joins). The transformed change subtracts the mean raw change of all states with a change in that year and divides by one pooled constant per lag and variable, the SD of the year-centred change over all rows. So zero is the average state's change over the same window and the unit is one pooled SD (constants in `data/change_scale.csv`, the manifest notes and the build log; M 0.130 / 0.193 / 0.236 / 0.317 and R_pr 0.091 / 0.154 / 0.198 / 0.277 log points for h = 1, 3, 5, 10). The same table feeds the initiator change in stage 1 (W and X) and the target change in stage 2. |
| Stage-2 target changes | The alternatives files carry `dM_j_h`, `dRpr_j_h` (the target's transformed change) and `dch_j_missing` (1 when the target has no position at t−1−h; its changes are then 0). `z_columns()` adds the two changes to Z in every model including M1m/M1r/M2m/M2r; `assemble_cell()` adds the indicator only when it is non-constant in the assembled cell and logs the decision (it enters in every main-grid cell). Pz rises from 6 to 9. The two changes are centred over the alternative rows like the other continuous Z columns; the indicator stays 0/1. |
| Assertions | Finite values everywhere; within-year mean of the transformed change 0 (1e-8); pooled SD 1 (1e-8); the panel's raw change equals its own level difference; the target change equals the initiator change of the same state-year wherever both exist; and |cor| < 0.02 between the transformed change and the 3-year log growth in the number of states on the panel rows. Logged before/after at h = 3 (raw log change vs growth, M +0.08, R −0.25; transformed, 0.000 and 0.000; the z-score version was +0.29 / +0.17). |
| Not changed | Priors, both Stan programs, event and sample coding, sampling settings, typology classification rules, choice sets. Panel and alternative row counts are identical to the previous build. |
| Tests | New and updated tests in `test_data_coding.R` cover the log levels and log-ratio gaps, the PageRank guard, calendar-join raw changes (gap years dropped, not bridged), within-year centring over the states with a change, one pooled constant, the zero system-growth correlation, the target-change join, the missing-target indicator and its Z decision, the new `z_columns()`. 536 expectations pass; both dry runs (fmid, h = 3, M2b K = 2 and M1m) assemble with S = 217, N1 = 14,597, N2 = 1,723,559, E = 583, P = 6, Pz = 9. |

Scale flags for the PI (reported, not changed). The gaps are now dM with SD 3.25 (5/50/95 percentiles −5.4 / 0.0 / 5.5) and dR_pr with SD 0.51 (−0.7 / 0.0 / 0.8) at h = 3; the transformed changes have SD 1 by construction (dM_h −1.3 / 0.0 / 1.4, dRpr_h −0.8 / −0.1 / 1.0). Prior scales and thresholds set in SD units of the old positions now sit on log-ratio units. (a) μ0 ~ N(0, 2) (`prior_mu0_sd` in `build_stan_data()`) is no longer weak on the M axis relative to a gap SD of 3.2 and is loose on the R axis. (b) σ_x ~ lognormal(0, 1) in both Stan programs has its median at 1 against a dM spread of 3.2. (c) The typology radius r0 = 0.5 (`03_extract.R` default, `typology.R` bands) and the measurement-error deltas 0.5 / 1.0 (`run_measurement_real.R`) mean a 65% or 170% share ratio rather than half an SD. β, α ~ N(0, 1) on the changes and γ ~ N(0, 1) on the target changes are on unit-SD inputs and look fine. `sim/simulate.R` still generates z-scored positions.

### Addendum (Oct 23, 2026): one scale constant per gap axis

The log-ratio gaps had SD 3.27 (material) and 0.52 (relational), which left the σ_x and μ0 priors and the typology radius r0 mis-scaled and made the two axes incomparable in the joint models. PI chose option 1. Each gap is divided by one constant per axis, the SD of the within-year gap over all ordered pairs of distinct states (`gap_scale()`, exact formula, unit-tested against brute force), 3.268 for M and 0.524 for R_pr. The constant is the same for every year, lag and variant, so it does not reintroduce the system-growth artifact. Raw log ratios are kept as `dM_logratio` / `dR_pr_logratio` and the constants are in `change_scale.csv` (h = NA). Scaled gaps have SD 0.99 and 0.97 at h = 3. One unit is a 26-fold CINC ratio or a 1.7-fold PageRank ratio. Priors, Stan programs and r0 are unchanged. Tests 540 pass; dry runs (fmid, lag 3, M2b and M1m) assemble with S = 217, N1 = 14,597, N2 = 1,723,559, E = 583, Pz = 9.

### Deployment guards (Oct 23, 2026; from the independent audit of 888568f..d3bcbfc)

The audit found the three commits correct and flagged two deployment risks. (a) rstan silently ignores unused data, so an old compiled program could run on the new data without μ0. `02_fit_model.R` now stops unless the compiled program declares `mu0`, and `01_build_data.sbatch` stops (`set -eo pipefail`) if compilation fails before the build. (b) Old and new fits share cell names and pass the like-with-like checks. `assert_fit_current()` (00_helpers.R) now makes `03_extract.R` and `compare_models.R` stop when a fit's stored data digests differ from the current manifest, and every fit records the git commit. Audit hypothesis left for the PI: μ0 on the material axis may be weakly identified against the target intercepts b_j, since only about 4% of the variance in M is within-state over time (38% for R).

## Joiners and ongoing disputes (2026-10-24; PI-approved)

Problem. The event base counted only originator pairs, so a state that joined a war was neither an initiator nor a target, while the conflict-history controls could count joiners through `INCLUDE_JOINERS`. Stage 2 also treated a new dispute between two states already at war as a fresh target choice, although the initiator was not selecting among alternatives. The dyadic peace-years control and the absence of a lagged involvement term left the model without a direct measure of whether the pair, or the state, was fighting the year before.
