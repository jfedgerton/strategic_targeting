Interpretation: apply the closed triggers to the coalition's principal contemporaneous public justification, not my judgment of the justice of the intervention. Ordinary collective-defense rhetoric is not automatically an excluded “alliance-war aim” when the specific public rationale is repelling identified aggression; otherwise Korea and the Gulf War would also be excluded. Accept the sheet's supplied MID force counts provisionally, flag contrary or incomplete historical evidence, and do not treat deploying troops, supplying bases or signing an alliance as necessarily using force. For a MID spanning operations, a yes is a dispute-level classification, not a claim that every operation or participant meets the rule. UN authorization concerns that coalition operation, not subsequent peacekeeping, an embargo alone, or recognition of self-defense.

URLs below identify primary documents or repositories of contemporaneous documents. Where a cited collection/document does not establish a main public justification or actual force count, the note says so. Borderline means unresolved evidence or a material rule boundary, not an instruction to include it in a strict list.
