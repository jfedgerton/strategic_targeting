# History-chosen dispute exclusions — DRAFT for PI approval (2026-11-19)

These lists are chosen on historical grounds, independently of any estimate. They are separate from the influence-chosen top-5 and top-10 drops, which are deliberately adversarial. Nothing here runs until the lists are approved and saved as `inputs/exclusions_un.txt` and `inputs/exclusions_punitive.txt` (one dispute number per line, then a space and a comment). An event is dropped if any of its source disputes is listed.

## Coding rule (fixed before running)
**Collective punitive intervention.** A coalition of three or more states uses force against a target. The coalition's stated public justification is the target's violation of an international norm or obligation: aggression against another state, mass atrocities, attacks on foreign nationals or diplomats, or defiance of UN Security Council resolutions. Territorial, colonial, succession and alliance-war aims do not qualify.

**UN-authorized subset.** The coalition's use of force was authorized by the UN Security Council.
